RTL Support

Automatic right-to-left detection for Persian, Arabic, Hebrew, and Urdu. When a document contains enough RTL characters, Markup+ switches the entire layout.

Why auto-detect?

You don't need special syntax. Write Persian or Arabic normally — Markup+ detects the direction automatically and adjusts fonts, alignment, and layout.

How detection works

Markup+ counts RTL characters in the document. If 30% or more of the letter characters are RTL, the entire document becomes RTL.

RTL charactersResult
Less than 30%LTR (default)
30% or moreRTL automatically

Manual direction control

You can override auto-detection with the direction option:

OptionEffect
direction: "rtl"Force right-to-left
direction: "ltr"Force left-to-right
direction: nullAuto-detect (default)
Note

Force LTR when a document has an English title but Persian body. Force RTL when the Persian content is less than 30% but you still want RTL layout.
