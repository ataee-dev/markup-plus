Math

Render mathematical formulas with KaTeX. Supports both inline math (inside a sentence) and block math (centered on its own line).

Why KaTeX?

KaTeX renders math fast and looks identical across browsers. It's loaded from CDN when includeCDN: true.

Inline math

Wrap math in single dollar signs to render it inside a sentence: $E = mc^2$.
